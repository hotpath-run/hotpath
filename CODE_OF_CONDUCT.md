# Code of Conduct

We want Hotpath to be a place where people can ask questions, report problems and send changes without being treated badly. This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and any space that represents the project.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and assume good faith. Disagree with the idea, not the person.
- Review kindly and specifically: say what to change and why.
- Welcome newcomers. A question that looks basic is a hole in the docs.
- Respect privacy. Do not share another person's private information, and redact traces and workflows before you post them: they can contain real data.

## What we do not accept

- Harassment, insults, threats, discrimination, or sexualised language or images.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or pushing again after someone has said no.
- Publishing someone's private information without their consent.
- Spam, or promoting unrelated products.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads, and block people who break these rules, and they will say why when they can. To report a problem, open the repository's **Security** tab, choose **Report a vulnerability** (it is private to the maintainers), and start the report with "Conduct:". Reports are handled privately and in good faith.

This code follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
